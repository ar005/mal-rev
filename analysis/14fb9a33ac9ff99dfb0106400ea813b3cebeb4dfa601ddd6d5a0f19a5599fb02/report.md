Instead of standard conditional jumps, the packer performs a calculation, and the *result* of that math (the carry bit or whether a value exceeds 0xd5) determines the next block of code. This forces both human analysts and automated tools to evaluate the entire mathematical chain to know which path the execution will take.
*   **The "Gatekeeper" Constants:** The constants `0x2a` and `0xd5` appear as threshold gates. They are used in conjunction with `CONCAT` operations to ensure that manipulated values fall into specific bit-ranges required by the CPU's instruction decoder. By rotating bits of a known sequence, the packer ensures that even if an analyst sees a piece of data in memory, it doesn't look like code until it is rotated at the exact moment of execution.
